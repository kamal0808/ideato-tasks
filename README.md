# Ideato tasks

Open tasks for [Ideato](https://www.ideato.social). Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns.

## How it works

1. Pick any open issue. Anyone can hand in, and the best hand-in wins. A `winners:N` label means up to N people can each earn the Neki.
2. Do the work however you like, including with your own AI.
3. Hand in by commenting on the issue with a link others can open and a line or two on what you did. No GitHub account? Comment on [Ideato's idea page](https://www.ideato.social/idea/b5e8c42c-ad2d-42de-9ead-a6f981e74c2a) for now.
4. Kamal accepts the best hand-in, labels the issue `accepted`, and closes it. The Neki is recorded on Ideato.

## Rules

- Proof must be something others can open, and anyone you quote or record has to agree to it.
- Made-up people, answers or numbers earn nothing.
- Neki is a record of your work and becomes your share in what gets built. Details: Ideato's [project brief](https://www.ideato.social/briefs/ideato.md).
